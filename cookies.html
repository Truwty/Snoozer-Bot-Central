Operator review required. This operational policy is a draft for operator review, not legal advice.

This site sets no cookies.

The Snoozer Bot Central website sets no HTTP cookies and uses no localStorage, sessionStorage, or other persistent browser storage. It has no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking content. No optional tracking cookies or similar technologies are present, so there is no “accept all” choice.

Notice dismissal

The on-page cookie information notice can be dismissed for the current page. Dismissal changes only the visible page in memory; the site does not save a consent or dismissal preference. The notice may appear again when you navigate to another page or reload.

Third-party hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process ordinary request or security logs under GitHub's own terms and arrangements. This is separate from cookies or browser storage set by the Snoozer Bot Central website.

Contact and draft status

The public-facing operator name is Snoozer Bot Central. The stated contact route is the Snoozer Bot Central Yapper community. This policy is an operational draft for review by the operator and is not legal advice.
